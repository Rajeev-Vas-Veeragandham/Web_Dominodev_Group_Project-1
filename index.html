Margherita Pizza

Prep Time 20 mins
Cook Time 25 mins
Difficulty Easy
Serves

Ingredients

  • 125g pizza dough
  • 50ml tomato sauce
  • 75g fresh mozzarella
  • 250g pizza dough
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  • 150g fresh mozzarella
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 200ml tomato sauce
  • 300g fresh mozzarella
  • 750g pizza dough
  • 300ml tomato sauce
  • 450g fresh mozzarella
  • 10 fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Instructions

1

Prepare the Dough

Preheat oven to 240°C (465°F). Roll out pizza dough on a floured surface to 30cm diameter.

2

Add Sauce & Cheese

Spread tomato sauce evenly, leaving 2cm border. Top with sliced mozzarella.

3

Bake Pizza

Bake for 12-15 minutes until crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.

4

Finish & Serve

Remove from oven, top with fresh basil leaves, drizzle with olive oil, and slice.

Pepperoni Classic Pizza

Prep Time 25 mins
Cook Time 20 mins
Difficulty Easy
Serves

Ingredients

  • 125g pizza dough
  • 250g pizza dough
  • 500g pizza dough
  • 750g pizza dough
  • 50ml tomato sauce
  • 100ml tomato sauce
  • 200ml tomato sauce
  • 300ml tomato sauce
  • 75g pepperoni
  • 150g pepperoni
  • 300g pepperoni
  • 450g pepperoni
  • 100g mozzarella cheese
  • 1 tsp oregano

Instructions

1

Prepare Base

Preheat oven to 230°C (450°F). Roll out dough and place on pizza stone.

2

Add Toppings

Spread sauce, add cheese, and arrange pepperoni slices evenly.

3

Bake to Perfection

Bake for 15-18 minutes until crust is crispy and cheese is golden.

4

Serve Hot

Sprinkle with oregano and slice while hot. Enjoy immediately!
